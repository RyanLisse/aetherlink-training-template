## Outcome after the series

Participants can frame an outcome, work with an agent across Plan, Design, Build, Test, Deploy, and Maintain, review changes, and hand work over with evidence another person can freshly verify. Each session produces a concise recap and at least one candidate reusable lesson when learning is validated.

## Success checks

- [ ] The cohort can state an outcome and an observable acceptance check.
- [ ] Each live exercise names a human owner, agent operator, reviewer, and evidence owner.
- [ ] The cohort distinguishes a claim, a source, a reproduced check, and a hypothesis.
- [ ] A receiver can follow a handoff and confirm the current state without relying on the sender's memory.
- [ ] Recaps lead to reviewed knowledge notes rather than a diary of activity.
